# Planetary Solipsism

Imagine a universe in which every person inhabits their own **Planet Earth**. Not metaphorically — literally. Every continent, ocean, city, and star exists for you alone, and only for you. Within the bounds of your planet, **true solipsism reigns**: everything that exists does so only because you perceive it, interact with it, or will it into being. Mountains rise and rivers flow according to your attention. Cities appear where you wish them to, histories unfold as you remember them, and all other inhabitants — if they appear at all — are phenomena entirely under the domain of your consciousness.

Yet this is not a universe of isolation. In the broader cosmos, **other planets exist**, each equally solipsistic and independently complete. Your parents, friends, and strangers inhabit their own fully-realized Earths, each governed by their own perception and will. **No mind crosses into another’s planet**, and no object exists outside the frame of its native consciousness. The solipsism within each planet is absolute, yet pluralism governs the universe: multiple Earths, multiple perspectives, all coexisting, never colliding.

If communication is to occur between these planets, it must respect the boundaries of solipsism. Nothing from one planet can directly enter another; only **structured signals** traverse the void. A message from your mother’s Earth does not contain your mother herself. It arrives as a pattern, a symbolic packet, a formal structure that your planet interprets and integrates. Identity, meaning, and continuity emerge **locally**, inferred from the consistency of these signals, not imposed by an external authority. In this way, relationships, collaboration, and trust are possible without ever violating the internal solipsistic law of each planet.

What exists beyond the planets themselves is more enigmatic. It is not a shared world, not a God’s-eye perspective, nor a repository of objects. Rather, it is the **meta-constraint system** that enables structured signals to travel and be recognized. A kind of cosmic grammar or protocol space, the “outside” provides the rules that allow planetary communication without ever merging the realities themselves. This space is minimal yet essential — the scaffolding that makes interaction possible, without dissolving the absolute solipsism of each planet.

Planetary Solipsism reframes our understanding of subjectivity and reality. Each person is at the center of their own universe, free to shape existence within it, yet capable of meaningful engagement with others through structured communication. Solipsism is no longer a philosophical trap; it is a **fundamental design principle**, enabling pluralistic, distributed, and self-contained realities to coexist. Perhaps, in embracing the totality of one’s own planet, while acknowledging the existence of countless others, we may finally reconcile the solitary and social dimensions of consciousness.
